Fraud at FLiiTS: a line you never cross

A fundamental rule that protects both the operation and you personally. With an order in your hands, what counts is what actually happens, not what the app shows.

🚫 Never mark an order as delivered if you have not actually delivered it.

What is considered fraud

🔴 Fake delivery: marking the order as delivered without having delivered it.
🔴 Customer absent: pretending the customer is not there so you can keep the order.
🔴 Cancellation after pickup: keeping the order once it has been picked up, even if the customer is the one who ends up cancelling it.

Why it is so serious

For you: it can mean immediate removal from the platform, as well as legal and employment consequences.
For your colleagues and the team: it damages FLiiTS’s reputation with customers and platforms and puts the operation in your city at risk, which everyone’s work depends on.
For the customer: it creates a bad experience and complaints that affect the whole operation.

💡 Problem with a delivery? Wrong address, customer unavailable, blocked access… None of these situations justifies marking the order as delivered. Always contact the support of the platform you are working with at that moment before making any decision: they will help you solve it properly.
