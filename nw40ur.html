FLiiTS mein Fraud

Hum aapko ek bahut zaroori niyam yaad dilana chahte hain, jo team aur aap dono ki hifazat karta hai.

🚫 Kisi bhi haalat mein order ko "delivered" mark na karein, agar aapne use sach mein deliver nahi kiya hai.

Yeh harkatein fraud maani jaati hain aur inke gambhir nateeje hote hain:

🔴 Jhoothi delivery: order deliver kiye bina use delivered mark karna.
🔴 Customer absent: jhooth-mooth dikhana ki customer maujood nahi hai aur order apne paas rakh lena.
🔴 Pickup ke baad cancellation: order pick karne ke baad use apne paas rakh lena, jiske baad customer order cancel kar deta hai.
Aapke liye: aapko turant platform se hataya ja sakta hai, aur kanooni aur naukri se jude nateeje ho sakte hain.
Team ke liye: customers aur platforms ke saamne FLiiTS ki reputation kharab hoti hai, jisse aapke shehar mein operation khatre mein pad jaata hai.
Customer ke liye: bura experience aur complaints hoti hain, jo poore operation par asar daalti hain.

💡 Agar kisi delivery mein problem ho — galat address, customer available nahi, entry band — koi bhi faisla lene se pehle hamesha Support se contact karein. Jo order deliver nahi hua, use kabhi delivered mark na karein.

Zone Gating: apne zone mein hi connect karein

*Yeh niyam sirf Uber platform par laagu hota hai.

Service sahi tarah chalne ke liye ek zaroori niyam: apni shift ke dauraan aapko sirf usi zone mein connect karna hai jo aapko assign kiya gaya hai.

📍 Assign kiye gaye zone ke bahar kaam karna absence (gair-haaziri) maana jaata hai, chahe aap connected aur delivery ke liye available hon. Iske alawa, orders milne mein mushkil hoti hai, jisse aapki performance kam ho jaati hai.
