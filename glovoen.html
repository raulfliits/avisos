TWO NEW

Both of them protect you.

Hello FLiiTER:

Glovo is activating two checks in the app: a QR code at pickup and a PIN at delivery. Both protect you: with the QR you avoid taking the wrong bag, and with the PIN you avoid unfair customer claims.

📷 QR AT ORDER PICKUP

You scan instead of comparing by eye

1️⃣ On the order screen, tap “Scan QR code”.
2️⃣ Point the camera at the QR on the bag or the receipt: it turns green if it is your bag and red if it is not.
3️⃣ When the app confirms it is yours, “Confirm pickup” is unlocked.

• If the QR doesn’t work: tap “Enter PIN” and enter the last 2 digits of the shipment ID. If that doesn’t work either, contact support.
• When does it apply? Only with some partners. The app will tell you when you need to scan.
• It adds no time to the pickup: it replaces the manual check you already did. The scan is done on your phone and is not recorded alongside your ID.

🔐 PIN AT ORDER DELIVERY

The customer gives you a 4-digit code

1️⃣ The PIN is generated automatically when you pick up the order from the partner.
2️⃣ At the door, ask the customer for their code: they have it in the Glovo app, in the notification or in the Dynamic Island of their phone.
3️⃣ Enter it in the app to unlock “Confirm delivery”.

• When does it apply? Only on orders with a higher risk of ending up as undelivered, for example high-value orders or orders from new customers.
• Without the correct PIN, the confirm delivery button stays locked.

WHY THEY EXIST

They protect you. With the delivery confirmed, no customer can claim that an order you did deliver “never arrived”. And with the QR you make sure you take the right bag.
