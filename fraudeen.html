Hello FLiiTER:

At FLiiTS we want delivering to be fair and reliable for everyone who works honestly. That is why we are strengthening fraud controls during deliveries: we want to take better care of honest Fliiters. We will explain what changes and what counts as fraud.

OUR GOAL

Reliable, safe and fair deliveries, and taking better care of the Fliiters who deliver honestly.

WHAT WE CONSIDER FRAUD

Some examples

• Marking an order as delivered without having delivered it.
• Manipulating or faking your location (GPS) during delivery.
• Giving false information about an issue with the customer or the order.
• Renting, handing over or sharing your account with another person (account renting).
• Cancelling or reporting as undelivered an order that could have been delivered.
• Any other misuse of the platform to obtain a benefit.

WHAT THIS MEANS

From now on, fraud detected during deliveries will be taken into account and will have a negative effect on your score and your performance.

🤝 WHEN THE CUSTOMER IS THE ONE COMMITTING FRAUD

Every case will be assessed fairly

We understand that sometimes it is the customer who commits the fraud. That is why each case will be assessed individually, internally and together with the platform where the incident took place.

At FLiiTS we prioritise taking care of our Fliiters and treating every incident fairly.

✅ Cases that qualify, once validated, will not count towards your score.

If you find yourself in a situation like this, let us know as soon as possible through the usual support channels so we can review it with the full context.

IF YOU DELIVER HONESTLY

Nothing changes for you. These controls are designed to protect you: so that other people’s fraud does not harm those who do it right. Thank you for doing it right every day.
