📲 Uber Eats: log out when your shift ends

Uber Eats does not log you out automatically when your shift ends: you are the one who must log out. With the latest app update you no longer need to use the red hand button; the procedure is now this:

Step 1. Open the menu. Tap the “Menu” icon, in the bottom right corner of the screen.

Step 2. Log out. Scroll to the bottom and tap “Log out”. This way you stop receiving orders.

⚠️ Important: it is not enough to close the app or lock your phone: you must expressly choose “Log out” in the menu.

📋 Orders outside your shift: due to the changes in the app, provisionally, orders that come in outside your shift and are reassigned will be removed, without affecting your weekly performance. Orders that come in during your assigned hours must be completed as usual. Any problems caused in these first days will be resolved without negatively affecting the performance of any FLiiTer.

💬 Do you have questions or any problem with this procedure? Contact the FLiiTS support team.
