Hello FLiiTER:

Uber is rolling out contactless payment at the door. To receive these orders, all of us need to have the payments app set up on our phone.

MANDATORY FOR THE WHOLE FLEET

An NFC-compatible device and the app set up

Practically all current phones are NFC-compatible.

⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT

Delays and operational incidents caused by not having the app set up will not be justified.

💡 WHAT IS IT?

It is a new way for customers to pay: they will be able to pay at the door with their phone or card, holding it near your phone. It is expected to drive growth and bring more orders to the market.

It costs you nothing: Uber covers all processing costs. The payment goes directly to Uber and your earnings reach your Uber account, just like a regular prepaid order.

REGISTRATION

Do it now: less than 2 minutes

BEFORE YOU START

What you need on your phone

01 Your device must be NFC-compatible.
02 During registration and payment collection these settings must be on:
▫️ NFC (Android only): ON
▫️ Automatic date and time (Android and iOS): ON
▫️ Passcode or phone PIN (Android and iOS): ON
03 Update your phone and the Uber app to the latest version.
04 Open the setup link on your phone and set up the app.

STEP-BY-STEP REGISTRATION

Five steps and you’re done

01 Open the link on your phone. You will land on a page with two steps: download the payments app and connect it to your Uber account. No sign-in is required.
02 Download the payments app (Adyen). When you tap download, you will go to the app store.
03 When the download finishes, go back to the page and tap “Finish setup”.
04 Wait for it to complete. Keep the screen open; you don’t have to enter any information.
05 Tap open the Uber app. You will see the message that you can now accept contactless payments.

DURING THE ORDER

Contactless payment orders are clearly identified in the app and the rest of the experience is the same as always. If you have not set up the app yet, you will be reminded at the door, and it is required to complete the delivery.

AT THE DOOR

How to collect

01 You will see the exact amount the customer must pay. Tap “Collect payment”.
02 The app takes you automatically to the payments app.
03 Ask the customer to hold their card or phone near yours, close to your NFC reader.
04 The customer may need to enter their PIN on your phone.
05 If it works, you will see the approval confirmation. You can now complete the order.

POSSIBLE SITUATIONS

• The payment fails: ask the customer to try again and tap “Collect payment” once more.
• It keeps failing or they can’t pay contactless: tap “There was an issue”. The customer switches their payment method in their app to a prepaid one. If they want to pay with PayPal, it is the same option.
• They want to pay in cash: it is not an option in Spain. They must choose a prepaid digital payment; otherwise they stay in debt and pay later.
• They don’t answer or have no payment method: tap “The customer is not responding”. You complete the order, receive your payment and the customer stays in debt.

TIPS TO MAKE IT WORK

• Find your NFC reader: on iPhones it is usually near the top; on Android, in the middle or at the top, depending on the model.
• If they pay with their phone: they should hold it against the back or top of yours; both readers must be together.
• If they pay with a card: flat and horizontal against your phone, near the reader.
• No quick taps: keep the card or phone close and still for a few seconds. If it isn’t detected, adjust the position slowly.
• If it doesn’t work: don’t screen-record or keep the camera open, don’t pull the card away too soon, remove thick phone cases and, if it still fails, tap “There was an issue”.

SETUP PROBLEMS

• NFC on? Only NFC phones can collect payments. On Android, check it.
• PIN or passcode on the phone? It is essential to finish the setup. Turn it on and try again.
• Time set to automatic? It cannot be manual.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which payments are accepted? Contactless credit or debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and other compatible digital wallets.

Where do my earnings go? The payment goes straight to Uber and your earnings reach your Uber account.

Do I pay any fee? No, Uber covers all costs.

Can I collect cash? No, it is not an option in Spain.

Who must register? The whole fleet: it is mandatory.
